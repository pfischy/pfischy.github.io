# AGENTS.md

## Product
FISCH is Patrick Fischer's consulting and creative systems website.

## Current architecture
- Public static website.
- This is the live front door for the FISCH brand.
- Protect existing deployment and custom domain behavior.

## Priorities
1. Preserve the live site.
2. Make surgical, high-confidence changes.
3. Improve positioning, clarity, conversion, and credibility.
4. Preserve mobile behavior, SEO metadata, links, forms, and domain-related files.
5. Avoid generic agency language.

## Editorial voice
- Use **we/our** for FISCH's offers, commitments, and advice. Avoid switching to **I/my** in company-facing site copy.
- The About page describes Patrick in the third person and explicitly identifies FISCH as founder-led. Do not imply a large team or claim employer work as FISCH client work.
- Visitor-facing self-check statements and visitor-authored prefilled inquiry emails may use **I/my** because they speak for the visitor, not FISCH.
- Prefer clear active sentences to sentence fragments and generic consulting language.

## Rules
- Do not remove CNAME or deployment-related files.
- Do not introduce a framework without approval.
- Do not rewrite the entire site unless explicitly asked.
- Work in small changes.
- Explain what changed and what should be checked after deployment.
