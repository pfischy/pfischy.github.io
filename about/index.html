About the founder

Built by someone who still does the work.

FISCH is the independent practice of Patrick Fischer, a learning and development leader who designs and builds practical learning, tools, and systems.

How we think

We don't build training for training's sake.

Our approach comes from working inside organizations where handoffs break: important knowledge sits in too few heads, new systems go unused, and courses stop before the work begins.

Patrick's career spans learning leadership, product design, organizational enablement, brand strategy, and creative production. He currently serves as Senior Manager, Learning & Development at Evergreen Nephrology. Earlier experience includes General Mills, Siemens Healthineers, and Strategic Education.

FISCH is Patrick's independent consulting and building practice. We help clients turn useful expertise into practical systems, bringing strategic direction and hands-on production together.

Organizations named here are employers or past employers, not presented as FISCH consulting clients or endorsers. Client work and internal employment experience are distinct.
