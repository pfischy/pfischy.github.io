# Make It Repeatable — founder video system

## Public positioning
Patrick Fischer: working L&D leader + hands-on builder. Internal corporate roles demonstrate practitioner experience; only independently contracted work counts as FISCH client work.

## Minimum effective cadence
- Every 2 weeks: one 75–120 second practical video showing a real process problem and a concrete intervention.
- Every week: one independent LinkedIn field note (100–250 words) with a point of view, an example, and a question.
- Each month: one usable original resource (worksheet, scenario, or simple tool).
- Each week: five targeted warm relationship conversations; not five generic pitches.
- Every month: review qualified meetings, proposals, signed work, and retained revenue—not likes.

## Video 01 — "They don't need another explanation" (90 seconds)

0:00–0:12 [Camera / talk directly]
"Here's something I see constantly at work: when someone makes a mistake, the response is to explain the process again. And again. And again."

0:12–0:25 [Show a realistic fabricated SOP / generic slide]
"But if the person already knows the rule and still gets stuck in the real moment, more information won't fix it."

0:25–0:48 [Screen-share an original, fabricated example]
"Let's say a customer handoff keeps failing. Instead of a 20-slide refresher, build a one-screen handoff guide: what a good handoff contains, one completed example, and a quick check before it gets sent."

0:48–1:05 [Point to a mock prompt near the work]
"Put it where the handoff happens. Ask a manager to review two real handoffs this week. Capture one signal: did rework go down?"

1:05–1:20 [Camera]
"That's the difference between training people *about* the work and building something that makes the work repeatable."

1:20–1:30 [End card]
"Make it repeatable. FISCH. fisch.life. Grab the free checklist in the Field Guide."

## Video 02 — "Is it training or a broken default?"
Pick an original, invented process (never confidential employer footage). Show the old multi-step habit and the one-click prompt/checklist that removes friction. End with the free Does This Need Training tool.

## Video 03 — "When is a new hire actually ready?"
Show a before: 12 completed modules. After: three role-specific demonstrations, a manager checklist, and an observed example. End with the Onboarding Readiness Map.

## Production rules
- Film human, not polished to death. One camera, good sound, confident 90 seconds.
- Record original and synthetic demo data only. Never use employer screenshots, internal policies, or confidential procedures.
- One clear hook, one example, one outcome. No long intro, no AMES framework lecture.
- Publish to Patrick's professional LinkedIn, embed or link on FISCH, and repurpose a 20–30 second clip if useful.
- Attribute job titles and corporate history accurately. Avoid borrowed employer logos and unaudited savings claims.
