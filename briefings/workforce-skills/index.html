What we'll cover

Five questions. One better training plan.

This isn't a grant seminar. It's a practical way to turn a skills challenge into something an employer can teach, support, and measure.

01Which job behavior must change?+

Start with one real task, not a broad topic. “Conduct a complete shift handoff” can be observed; “Improve communication” cannot.

Example: Name the task, role, moment, and success condition.

02Where will employees practice it?+

Use a realistic choice or task before revealing a model answer. Ask someone to act, not just recall information.

Example: Two-minute handoff scenario with consequence-based feedback.

03What support exists when work gets difficult?+

Identify the job aid, example, prompt, supervisor conversation, or tool that will still be there after the workshop.

Example: Four-field handoff template embedded in the normal shift log.

04Who reinforces the new behavior?+

Equip a manager or lead with a clear observation prompt and one conversation to have during the first week.

Example: Check two handoffs for clear owner, impact and checkpoint.

05What evidence shows it's working?+

Choose leading evidence from the work and one or two downstream measures. Review early signs within 14 days where practical.

Example: Fewer ambiguous handoffs and fewer preventable follow-up calls.
