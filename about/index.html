About the founder

Built by someone who still does the work.

I'm Patrick Fischer. I lead learning and development inside organizations and independently build learning, tools, and systems through FISCH.

My perspective

I'm not interested in training for training's sake.

I've worked where the handoffs break: teams with too much knowledge in too few heads, new systems no one uses, and courses that stop before the real work begins.

My career spans learning leadership, product design, organizational enablement, brand strategy, and creative production. My current role is Senior Manager, Learning & Development at Evergreen Nephrology. Earlier experience includes General Mills, Siemens Healthineers, and Strategic Education.

FISCH is my independent consulting and building practice. I help clients turn useful expertise into practical systems. I bring both perspectives to the table: strategic operator and hands-on maker.

Organizations named here are employers or past employers, not presented as FISCH consulting clients or endorsers. Client work and internal employment experience are distinct.

Strategy. Design. Production. Implementation.

Systems thinking

A small, useful practice

I don't need to be a giant agency.

FISCH works best when the problem is real, the people are close to the work, and the goal is to build something that will actually get used. I work with clients directly, keep the scope clear, and build with the tools that make sense.
